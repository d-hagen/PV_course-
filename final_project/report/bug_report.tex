\section*{Summary}
Every general-case GCD computation produces the \emph{correct result} but asserts
\texttt{out\_valid} \textbf{one clock cycle too late}, violating the latency
specification. Early-exit operands ($a=0$, $b=0$, $a=b$) are unaffected.

\section*{Steps to Reproduce}
\texttt{make regress} (seeds 1 / 7 / 42). Failures appear in all three tests.

\section*{Observed vs.\ Expected}
848 of 1025 scoreboard checks fail. The result value always matches the reference;
the measured latency is always \texttt{expected + 1} for any operand pair that
enters the \texttt{RUN} state. Representative cases:

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{lcccc}
\toprule
Operands & \texttt{gcd\_out} (obs/exp) & Latency obs & Latency exp & Result \\
\midrule
$\gcd(6,9)$       & 3 / 3 & 4      & 3      & value OK, +1 cycle \\
$\gcd(3,5)$       & 1 / 1 & 5      & 4      & value OK, +1 cycle \\
$\gcd(65535,1)$   & 1 / 1 & 65536  & 65535  & value OK, +1 cycle \\
$\gcd(0,5)$       & 5 / 5 & 1      & 1      & \textbf{pass} (early-exit) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\section*{Detection}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
  \item \textbf{Scoreboard latency check (RQ-L02)} --- reports
        \texttt{delay = N+1, Expected delay = N} on every general-case transaction.
  \item \textbf{Assertion \texttt{A\_RUN\_CONVERGE}} (\texttt{tb\_gcd.sv:1324},
        \texttt{(a\_next==b\_next \&\& state==RUN) |=> state==DONE}) --- fired
        848 times.
\end{itemize}
Value-correctness, handshake, and reset checks all pass; the defect is purely
temporal, so a value-only testbench would not have caught it.

\section*{Root Cause}
The RUN$\rightarrow$DONE convergence test compares the \emph{registered} operands
instead of the \emph{combinational next-cycle} operands:

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\textbf{Buggy} & \texttt{if (a\_reg == b\_reg)} \\
\textbf{Correct} & \texttt{if (a\_next == b\_next)} \\
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\noindent Spec \S2.4 requires the termination check to be evaluated on the
next-cycle values; comparing the registered values detects convergence one cycle
late, so the FSM stays in \texttt{RUN} for one extra cycle before entering
\texttt{DONE}.

\section*{Proposed Fix}
\texttt{rtl/gcd.sv}, RUN-state convergence test: change
\texttt{if (a\_reg == b\_reg)} back to \texttt{if (a\_next == b\_next)}. One-token
change; restores \S2.5 latency ($1{+}N$ cycles) with no datapath impact.
